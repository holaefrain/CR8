CR8

Privacy

Short version: CR8 has no servers and collects nothing. Everything the app knows about you stays on your computer.

What the app stores on your computer

  • Your first name (optional) and Discogs username.
  • Your Discogs personal access token, encrypted with your operating system's keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows DPAPI).
  • Your collection: the tracks you add, with BPM, key and edits.
  • Your settings (which enrichment sources are on).

These live in CR8's app-data folder. Uninstalling CR8 and deleting that folder removes them. In the app you can remove your token at any time (Discogs tab → Remove) and export or clear your collection.

Where the app connects

  • Discogs (api.discogs.com) — search, your profile, wantlist and collection, using your token.
  • Beatport (beatport.com) — public search pages, to look up BPM and key. Can be turned off.
  • YouTube — release videos listed on Discogs, downloaded briefly to analyse BPM and key, then discarded. Can be turned off.
  • GitHub — to check for CR8 updates and to download the yt-dlp helper.
  • DYMO Connect on your own computer (127.0.0.1) — to print labels.

CR8 has no analytics, tracking or crash reporting.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Your host may keep standard server logs.
