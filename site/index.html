Questions

What is a Discogs token?

A personal access token lets apps use the Discogs API as you — to search the database and read your wantlist and collection. You generate one in Discogs → Settings → Developers. CR8 walks you through it.

Where is my token stored? Is it safe?

On your computer only, encrypted with your operating system's keychain (macOS Keychain or Windows DPAPI). CR8 has no servers: the token is only ever sent to Discogs. You can rotate or remove it anytime in the app.

Why does macOS ask about keychain access?

The first time CR8 saves your token, macOS may ask to let CR8 use its own "CR8 Safe Storage" keychain item. That item holds the key that encrypts your token.

Does it work with my LabelWriter?

CR8 prints through DYMO Connect to LabelWriter 450, 550, 4XL, 5XL and Wireless models, and can read the loaded roll on 550-series printers. You can also export a CSV for DYMO Connect, or an Excel sheet.

Is CR8 affiliated with Discogs?

No. CR8 is an independent app that uses the Discogs API. Data provided by Discogs.
